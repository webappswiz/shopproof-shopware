# 0.6.1
- For agencies and anyone running several shops: `bin/console shopproof:setup:check` prints the setup check from the Shopproof page (withdrawal button, page, form and confirmation email, button wording, product page layouts, manufacturers without details, guarantee notice). It ends with exit code 0 when everything checked is set up, 1 when something needs work and 2 when a part could not be checked; `--json` for scripts.
- `bin/console shopproof:settings:export` saves the Shopproof settings as a file, and `bin/console shopproof:settings:import <file>` applies it to another shop. Every value is checked against the settings first and nothing changes if one is wrong; `--dry-run` shows what would change, `--sales-channel` sets one sales channel.
- Rules list 0.11.0: new rules on the 14 day withdrawal right and its 12 month extension for the EU, Austria, Germany, France, Italy and the Netherlands. Every rule now links the law itself, or the body the law entrusts, as its source. The EU withdrawal button rule names Austria's later start (contracts from 1 October 2026).
- Checked before release (0.6.0 was never released on its own): the withdrawal form opened from the signed link is never kept in the page cache or a proxy, since it shows the customer's details. The item lookup allows 20 tries per connection in a fixed hour, then none until that hour is over. Ticked items that are not on the order are left out. Moving a withdrawal to Return arrived or Refund sent asks first, because it emails the customer. The command line setup check names an unusable returns tool address in words, and the settings export says it leaves out the excluded categories (their ids differ in every shop). Returns tool addresses in the NAT64 and 6to4 ranges are refused. The Return arrived and Refund sent emails and the shop copy's line on excluded items now also come in French, Italian and Dutch (picked by the language's locale, English for any other).

# 0.6.0
- Withdrawal link in the customer account: each order in "Orders" has "Withdraw from contract here" in its menu, next to "Repeat order". It opens the withdrawal form already filled in. Setting "Withdrawal link in the customer account", on by default.
- The withdrawal link in the order emails opens the form already filled in with the customer's name, email, order number and order date. The link carries the order's id and a signature made with the shop's own secret, never the email address; a changed or guessed link shows an empty form.
- New setting "Order date on the withdrawal form": not asked (default), optional or required. The date cannot be in the future and shows in the Shopproof list of withdrawal requests.
- Button wording picker: keep the national wording (default), use a reviewed alternative where the law of the language's country allows one (Dutch for now), or enter your own text for the footer button and the confirm button. Your own text applies only after you tick that you understand the legal wording; the setup check says when the chosen wording does not apply.
- Withdrawal status for the customer: each request in the Shopproof list has a status (Withdrawal received, Return arrived, Refund sent). Shopware's confirmation email links to a page where the customer sees it, and moving it on emails the customer ("Your return arrived", "Your refund is on its way"), through the shop's own mail setup. The words are Shopware email templates you can change under Settings > Email templates. When Shopware marks the order's payment as refunded in full, the status moves to Refund sent by itself. New setting to turn the emails off.
- Deadline clock in the list: whether a withdrawal came in time (14 days, or your longer period, from delivery, moved to Monday when it ends on a weekend) and when the refund is due (14 days after it arrived), with the days left or overdue. Enter the day the goods arrived; until then the shipping date stands in, which can only say "in time" or "check". Technical help, not legal advice.
- Returns tool: enter your returns portal and the withdrawal confirmation email says "Start your return here" with its link. Enter a webhook address and each new withdrawal goes to your returns tool or automation (Zapier, Make, n8n) as signed JSON, the same as from the WooCommerce plugin and the Shopify app. A tool that does not answer gets three more tries over six hours, then you get an email. The Shopproof page shows the signing secret, sends a test and shows for each withdrawal whether it arrived. Only https addresses on the internet are called.
- Item picker on the withdrawal form: once the customer has entered the order number and email (or opened the form from the order email or the customer account), the form lists the items of that order with their photos to tick, with how many. Nothing ticked means the whole order. The ticked items are on the record, in both withdrawal emails and in the webhook. Setting "Let customers tick the items they send back", on by default.
- Excluded products: pick the categories (sub-categories included) and whether digital products are excluded from withdrawal, and write the explanation customers see. The form still lists those items, marked with your explanation, and your copy of the withdrawal names them so you can check each case.
- Comment field: Shopware's comment field now says that no reason is needed. New setting to make it required or leave it out.

# 0.5.0
- Product safety sits as one line under the Add to cart button ("Product safety (manufacturer: Acme GmbH)") that opens to the details. The full block below the description is still available (setting "Where to show it").
- Hazard labelling for chemicals (CLP Article 48a): per product, pick the pictograms and the signal word and enter the hazard and precautionary statement codes. The product page shows the official texts in the shopper's language, always visible above the safety line. The Shopproof page lists codes that need your own words or are unknown.
- Registration numbers and take-back: enter your WEEE, battery, packaging, IDU or textiles numbers in the settings. They show as one small line in the footer, and products marked as electrical equipment or containing batteries explain how to return old devices and batteries.
- Order confirmation and shipping emails end with the withdrawal link: "Withdraw from contract here. Changed your mind? You can withdraw within 14 days.", linking to the page of Shopware's withdrawal button. New setting for the withdrawal period (14 to 365 days).
- Proof log: a dated, hash-chained record of what the shop showed (daily snapshot) and which withdrawals it received, with no customer names, email addresses or order numbers. Check the chain on the Shopproof page (it finds a changed entry, a removed newest or oldest entry and an emptied log) and download it as CSV. Kept 2 years by default.
- The durability label now shows as the small strip that opens the full label, as in the WooCommerce plugin and the Shopify app. Products set to "Full label" keep it. When Shopware's own durability label (6.7.14 and later) shows on a product, Shopproof leaves its label out, so there is never a second one.
- Rules list 0.10.0, which includes the second reviewed Dutch withdrawal wording ("hier de overeenkomst herroepen" with "herroeping bevestigen") from 0.9.1. The other 0.10.0 additions are Pro research and are not in this list.
- Fix: on older Shopware 6.7 versions the storefront showed text keys such as "shopproof.safety.heading" instead of the words, and the national withdrawal button wording did not apply. The texts now also ship for the usual locales (en-GB, de-DE, de-AT and others).
- 0.4.5 was never released; its changes are part of this version. Its changelog wrongly named rules 0.9.1.

# 0.4.4
- Rules list 0.9.0: new EU rule on hazard labelling for chemicals sold online (CLP Article 48a).
- Product safety: the manufacturer and the EU responsible person each come from one source, so a manufacturer's name is no longer shown with the shop-wide default address and email. The defaults apply to products without a Shopware manufacturer and to the manufacturer with the default name. A manufacturer without details shows its name only, and the Shopproof page lists such manufacturers. A product that names another manufacturer no longer borrows the Shopware manufacturer's address.
- Withdrawal requests that could not be recorded are counted, and the Shopproof page shows a red notice until you mark them as checked. The setup check confirms that the table for the records exists.
- Withdrawal requests match their order also when the shopper types "#", spaces or "Order" or "Bestellung" before the number.
- The setup check warns while the EU legal guarantee notice is off (required for goods from 27 September 2026).
- The rules list also leaves out rules waiting for a check, of low confidence or unverified, and rules without an https source link.

# 0.4.3
- Shopproof now ships with a copy of the rules list and contacts no server on install or activation, or when you open the Shopproof page.
- New setting "Download rule updates once a day" (Rule updates card), off by default. The daily rules update runs only when you turn it on.
- New button "Check for rule updates now" on the Shopproof page, for a one-off download.
- The description says the rules list is included and daily updates are optional.
